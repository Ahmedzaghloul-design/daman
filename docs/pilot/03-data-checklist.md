# Pilot data checklist — what we ask the factory for

*Matches the fields in the Daman app (Installations, Products, Receipts) and
the EU data model (spec F14). Ask only for what is listed.*

## A. Installation (one form)
- [ ] Installation name and address (city), operator (legal company name)
- [ ] Main activity (e.g. "Manufacture of cement and clinker")
- [ ] Reporting period for the pilot (from – to)

## B. Products sold to the EU
For each product:
- [ ] CN code (8 digits, from the customs declaration or the buyer)
- [ ] Tonnes produced in the period
- [ ] Tonnes exported to the EU in the period
- [ ] Process emissions, if the plant already calculates them (e.g. from
      clinker production for cement). If not available, the app marks the
      product "Incomplete" rather than guessing.
- [ ] Precursors bought in (e.g. clinker for cement grinding), if any

## C. Energy and fuel receipts for the period
- [ ] Diesel receipts ("سولار" on Egyptian receipts means diesel), in litres
- [ ] Natural gas invoices (m³)
- [ ] Heavy fuel oil (مازوت), coal, petcoke receipts (tonnes)
- [ ] Electricity bills (kWh)
- [ ] Electricity source: grid, a power purchase agreement, or produced on
      site (F14)

## D. People (for the workspace)
- [ ] Owner email (manages the team)
- [ ] Reviewer email(s) (approve receipts)
- [ ] WhatsApp numbers of people who will send receipts, if used
- [ ] Verifier email, only if the factory already works with one

## E. Before starting
- [ ] Consent letter signed (02-consent-and-data-request.md), after legal review
- [ ] Gemini billing on (real receipts are blocked on the free tier)
- [ ] Emission factors agreed with the verifier entered in Settings (the
      built-in factors are illustrative)
