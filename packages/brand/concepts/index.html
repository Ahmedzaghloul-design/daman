Four symbolic logo concepts

Each concept carries a specific meaning from what Daman does. Every logo has two forms: a 3D version for the website and presentations (drag it, hover over it, click it) and a flat version for browser tabs, invoices and documents.

PALETTE · NILE & DESERT GOLD

Colors

Color rules. Bright gold is for the logo and decorative highlights only; it is too light for small text on cream. Gold text uses dark gold #875F1C. Green, amber and red keep their meaning (verified, needs review, anomaly) and are never used as decoration. Every text color meets the 4.5:1 accessibility contrast standard. The EU flag stars are intentionally not used, so Daman never looks like an official EU body.
